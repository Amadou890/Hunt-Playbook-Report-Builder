Hunt Playbook & Report Builder

Pick a technique, work through the checklist, and turn your findings into a structured case report. Companion to the Splunk Threat Hunting Lab.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Copy your report before closing the page. Playbooks are starting points based on common analyst practice. Adapt them to your environment.

Paste into a .md file, a GitHub README, or a ticket. Only steps you ticked are listed as performed.

Recommended actions are suggestions for an analyst to evaluate, not automatic responses. The report reflects only what you enter.
